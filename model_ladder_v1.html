Why a new layer
The v12 ranking is sound (#1 pick 25.2% over 424 recent races). The old bet-selection layer was the leak: it won more often (29.5%) but at shorter prices ($3.56 avg) in the −10% graveyard, returning −7.3% at price / −6.1% at BSP — worse than blindly backing every #1 (−3.6%). Its lever, the overlay %, does not rank-order return. The ladder replaces selection + staking with evidenced value pockets; v12 scoring is unchanged.

The profit is almost entirely tiers 3 and 4 (+$6.3k of +$6.1k net — tier 2 is slightly negative). Tier 2 is the remaining ~breakeven coverage tier: the price of a bet in most soft-track races, and a small one. Every tier-4/3 cell held its sign in both independent halves of the window; tier 2 did not, which is why it carries the minimum stake. Why v1.2: the Saturday-metro leg of Rule C was +38% overall but VIC alone was −30% in both halves (claude/saturday_metro_finding.md), so VIC Saturday metro no longer qualifies for tier 4 (frozen tier 4 goes 498 → 488 bets, +19.5% → +22.0%). The score≥21 shadow comes from the 26 Oct finding that #1-score adds nothing to win rate between 12 and 20 (the market prices it) but 21+ is a real threshold. Why v1.1: the coverage tiers were keyed on the meeting's going, so within a track they were uniform by construction — a good-track day read as "all #2 at $10". Tier 1 was the weak, sign-flipping cell producing that; removing it costs no EV, drops turnover from $60.8k to $48.2k on the backtest, and lifts ROI from +10.6% to +12.7%. Coverage falls from 82% to 54%.

Caveats

Tiers came from cells validated on both halves, but they were chosen after seeing the pooled map — residual selection bias; the forward period is the real test. Soft is seasonal (51% of the window): tier 2 and much of tier 4 fade in a dry summer; the metro leg of Rule C and tier 3 are the all-season anchors. The $5–8 band is a hard cut on a smooth curve. All returns are at pre-race price / BSP; live execution may differ. The live bets' recorded bet_pnl does not reconcile with their BSP return — basis unverified, to be reconciled separately.
