1 · One bet per race
Rule. After the gates run, if more than one horse in a race is BET, keep the highest v12 (ties: shorter price) and demote the rest to WATCH with the reason OnePerRace (v38.18) behind <horse> | <original reason>. Applied in run_venue_v28 (morning load) and rescore_prices (price cycle).

Eight races is a small sample; the rule is adopted because the second horse was never the model's pick in the first place (Flemington R5 on Cup day was a ClassDrop promotion on the #2; Geelong R7 on 7 Nov the same). The demoted horse settles as a shadow, so the rule's cost is measurable.

2 · Race-day controls
Pace bias. race_days.pace_bias ∈ {−2, −1, 0, +1, +2}, set from a dropdown on the race card once two or three races have shown the pattern. dashboard-api adds it to v12 live: at ±2, L ±2 · P/OP ±1 · M ∓1 · BK/OM ∓2 (halved at ±1). The card, v12_rank and the ladder re-rank instantly; stored scores and classifier bets never move. On Cup day an on-pace +2 would have lifted Point Of Law and Sons And Lovers back above Trustyourinstinct without touching the pipeline.
Going. The same control bar changes condition/track_rating; the ladder reads it live. Stored v12 keeps the morning pace terms until scripts/rescore_condition.py --venue X --cond "good 4" re-scores that one venue (seconds, not a day re-run) — it writes the going, the score columns, and marks/adds bets and shadows under the same never-delete policy.
Logged. Every going or bias change lands in condition_log (time, old, new, source, note). The bias magnitudes are a guess — there is no in-running data to fit them — so the log is what lets us measure them after a month and decide whether leaders-hold bias is worth ±2 or something else.
3 · Shadows are never deleted
Bug. rescore_prices.py deleted any WATCH shadow whose gate stopped matching at the new prices. On 7 Nov that was 13 of the 16 morning shadows (ClassDrop at Rosehill, Hawkesbury, Hobart…), none of which settled — so every forward test on the tracker has been undercounting. Fix: shadow_bets.dropped_at / dropped_reason, parity with bets (v38.12): marked, kept, settled as filed. Shadows that qualify again are un-dropped.
