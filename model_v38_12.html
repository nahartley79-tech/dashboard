CalibProbShadow — logistic model on normalised market prob + v12 z-score + score≥20 + jockey/trainer SR, normalised within race; pick at edge ≥15%, price ≤$10. Goes-live rule for CalibProb: ≥25% win over ≥20 shadows and positive flat P&L.

Volume — the honest table
Jul 1 – Sep 12, 44 race days, flat $10. No v12-based volume rule beats the market: every v12 #1 = 39/day, −12%; v12 #1 & favourite = 22/day, −10%; v12 #1 & score ≥13 = 16/day, −9%; v12 #1 & score ≥16 = 8/day, −2% (positive Aug and Sep); score ≥20 any rank = 1.7/day, +32%; live book as bet = 3.6/day, −7%. Removing the gates does not reveal a profitable volume book underneath — the score only pulls ahead of the market at the top of its range. A calibrated probability with bets sized by edge is the only route to a positive 8–15/day book, and the shadow books exist to find out whether it gets there.
